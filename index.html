How it stays fair

  • One public rule. Pieces come from a fixed table of odds (below). The only adjustment is the solvable-tray guarantee: every new tray can be fully placed on your board, in some order. If a roll can't, it is rerolled. If you lose, it's because earlier placements boxed you in, never because the game decided it was your turn to lose.
  • Nothing else is read. The generator never uses your score, streak, play time, or history. There are no ads, so there is nothing to tune against.
  • Seeded and replayable. Every game has a seed. Each tray is rolled from the seed and its tray number, so players on the same seed get the same trays. They only differ when the guarantee rerolls.
  • Daily challenge. Everyone gets the same seed each day.
  • No ads, no continues, no purchases. Your score depends only on your placements.

Scoring

  • 10 points per square placed.
  • A move that clears lines scores 100 × lines bonus × streak.
  • Lines bonus for clearing at once: 1 line ×1, 2 lines ×3, 3 lines ×6, 4 or more ×10.
  • Streak: your first clear is ×1, then each clear in a row adds ×0.5 (×1.5, ×2, ×2.5 …). The dots show how many placements you have left to clear before the streak breaks (3 placements without a clear).
  • Emptying the whole board: +3,000.
