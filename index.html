How it stays fair

  • One public rule. Pieces come from a fixed table of odds (below). The only adjustment is the fit guarantee: every new tray has at least one piece that fits your board. If a roll has none, it is rerolled.
  • Nothing else is read. The generator never uses your score, streak, play time, or history. There are no ads, so there is nothing to tune against.
  • Seeded and replayable. Every game has a seed. Each tray is rolled from the seed and its tray number, so players on the same seed get the same trays. They only differ when the fit guarantee rerolls.
  • Daily challenge. Everyone gets the same seed each day.
  • No ads, no continues, no purchases. Your score depends only on your placements.

Scoring

  • 1 point per square placed.
  • Clearing lines: 10 × lines² (1 → 10, 2 → 40, 3 → 90 …), multiplied by your combo.
  • Combo goes up each time a placement clears. It resets if 3 placements in a row clear nothing.
  • Emptying the whole board: +300.
